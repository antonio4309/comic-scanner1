# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Whatnot sellers who deal in physical comics. They move fast — scanning a longbox between shows, checking last-sold prices on the fly, building a bulk upload CSV before going live. They know their inventory intimately and need a tool that respects that knowledge: no hand-holding, no unnecessary steps. Many work in poor lighting, on mobile or tablet, often standing up.

## Product Purpose

LongboxLens identifies comic books from cover photos using AI (Gemini), fetches UK eBay last-sold prices, and exports a Whatnot-ready bulk import CSV. It removes the grunt work of manual lookups and spreadsheet wrangling so sellers can focus on the sale. Success looks like a seller scanning 50 comics in under five minutes and walking into a show with a complete, priced inventory.

## Brand Personality

Obsessive, collector-grade, dark. Feels like it was built by someone who actually collects — tactile, serious, slightly cinematic. Precision instrument, not lifestyle app.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: cream backgrounds, blue accents, identical card grids, Notion/Linear clones. LongboxLens is a specialist tool, not another productivity app.
- Anything that feels like it was designed for a general audience or that hides its domain knowledge.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed is respect** — every interaction should feel instant or give honest feedback. Sellers are time-pressured; never make them wait without explanation.
2. **Specialist confidence** — use the vocabulary of the domain (newsstand, direct, CGC slab, key issue). Don't dumb it down for people who don't collect.
3. **Dark by necessity, not trend** — the dark theme isn't aesthetic decoration; it's readable under harsh show lighting and doesn't wash out on screens next to bright overhead lights.
4. **Density with clarity** — pack information (badges, prices, conditions) without creating visual noise. Every element must earn its space.
5. **No dead ends** — every error state, empty state, and loading state has a clear next action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Focus indicators must be visible (the dark theme makes this especially important — amber outlines on dark backgrounds). All async actions must have loading states; sellers may be on slow mobile connections. Touch targets 44×44px minimum — this is used on phones and tablets.
