CGC Grading Reference

      
    

    

      
GradeNameKey criteria

      
        
9.8NM/MNearly perfect. Flat, square, gloss fully intact. Minimal imperfections.

        
9.6NM+Almost perfect. Tiny handling defect or 1 very small corner blunt.

        
9.4NMFew minor defects. Slight spine stress lines OK. Colour gloss retained.

        
9.2NM–Slight blunting, 2–3 minor stress lines. Above average in every way.

        
9.0VF/NMSlight wear. Small corner blunts, 1–2 stress lines, minor staple discolouration.

        
8.5VF+Minor wear. Small creases, slight handling. Very light soil on cover.

        
8.0VFSlightly used. Minor staple stress, corner blunting. Still a nice copy.

        
7.5VF–Some wear. Small spine roll, visible creases. No significant defects.

        
7.0FN/VFAverage used. Spine roll, stress lines, minor soil. Still bright and flat.

        
6.0FNWell-read. Creases, spine roll, some colour loss. No major defects.

        
5.0VG/FNHeavy wear. Possible small tears or small water stain. Heavy soil OK.

        
4.0VGVery heavy wear. Staple rust, tape present, minor missing pieces.

        
2.0GDHeavy wear, tape repair, major water stain, spine split, soiling.

        
1.0FRVery heavily worn. Possible page loss, large pieces missing.

        
0.5PRMajor defects throughout. Readable only. Brittle pages, coverless.
