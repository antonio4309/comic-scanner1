SCAN PROTOCOL

            

              
01Remove from bag or sleeve before scanning — plastics distort grade read

              
02Place flat on a solid surface under natural or even artificial light

              
03Include the full spine in frame — spine wear is the primary grade indicator

              
04CGC slabs: frame the full slab label so cert ID is visible for census lookup

              
05Upload the highest resolution image available for best AI identification

              
06Variants and newsstand editions may differ in value — include cover corner in shot

CGC Grading Reference 

    

      
GradeNameKey Criteria

      
        
9.8NM/MNearly perfect. Flat, square, gloss fully intact.

        
9.6NM+Almost perfect. Tiny handling defect.

        
9.4NMFew minor defects. Slight spine stress OK.

        
9.2NM–Slight blunting, 2–3 minor stress lines.

        
9.0VF/NMSlight wear. Small corner blunts.

        
8.0VFSlightly used. Minor staple stress.

        
7.0FN/VFAverage used. Spine roll, stress lines.

        
6.0FNWell-read. Creases, spine roll.

        
4.0VGVery heavy wear. Staple rust, tape.

        
2.0GDHeavy wear, tape repair, water stain.

        
1.0FRVery heavily worn. Possible page loss.

        
0.5PRMajor defects. Brittle pages.
