GradeNameKey Criteria

      
        
9.8NM/MNearly perfect. Flat, square, gloss fully intact.

        
9.6NM+Almost perfect. Tiny handling defect or 1 very small corner blunt.

        
9.4NMFew minor defects. Slight spine stress lines OK.

        
9.2NM–Slight blunting, 2–3 minor stress lines.

        
9.0VF/NMSlight wear. Small corner blunts, 1–2 stress lines.

        
8.5VF+Minor wear. Small creases, slight handling.

        
8.0VFSlightly used. Minor staple stress, corner blunting.

        
7.5VF–Some wear. Small spine roll, visible creases.

        
7.0FN/VFAverage used. Spine roll, stress lines, minor soil.

        
6.0FNWell-read. Creases, spine roll, some colour loss.

        
5.0VG/FNHeavy wear. Possible small tears or small water stain.

        
4.0VGVery heavy wear. Staple rust, tape present.

        
2.0GDHeavy wear, tape repair, major water stain.

        
1.0FRVery heavily worn. Possible page loss.

        
0.5PRMajor defects throughout. Brittle pages.
